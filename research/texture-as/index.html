Texture analysis / synthesis

Deep-Learned Texture Analysis/Synthesis Finally Beats Traditional Techniques

Zhiwei Xu, Huib de Ridder, Gaurav Sharma, Thrasyvoulos N. Pappas

Northwestern University · University of Rochester

  • arXiv — coming soon

Overview

Texture analysis/synthesis (A/S) maps a texture to a compact representation, then regenerates a perceptually equivalent texture from it. For years, the classical Portilla–Simoncelli (PS) model has been the bar that GAN-based A/S methods could not reach.

Our StyleGAN3-based framework combines a new dataset of 6,466 homogeneous textures, a texture-dependent loss that balances Sliced Wasserstein Distance and LPIPS per image, and retrieval-initialized multi-start latent optimization. In blind human comparisons, it was preferred over or tied with PS 92.4% of the time, while keeping a compact, manipulable latent code.

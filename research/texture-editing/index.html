Overview

We edit how a natural texture looks: we continuously increase or decrease a requested attribute (glossiness, roughness or surface relief) while keeping the texture's identity and natural appearance, and changing other attributes as little as possible.

Textures are reconstructed with a texture-trained StyleGAN3, refined with image-specific generator weight offsets, and edited in StyleSpace. Vision–language models give a natural way to describe the attribute, but their responses entangle unrelated properties; maximizing CLIP similarity to "glossy", for example, can shift hue instead of adding gloss. A single fixed direction also has a limited useful range. We therefore model each edit as a conditional editing flow: an image- and attribute-specific trajectory guided by a graph of attribute associations from CLIP, calibrated by StyleSpace interventions.

Each example below is a nine-step trajectory from −4 to +4; 0 is the unedited reconstruction. Drag a slider, or press Play, to move all ten examples of that attribute together.
